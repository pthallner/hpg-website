Firm Evaluation

The Firm Evaluation is a six-week structural assessment of your firm. It ends with a written report that belongs to the firm.

Length
Six weeks
Fee
$15,000, fixed
Led by
Paul Thallner
You receive
A written report the firm owns
Who it is for

Firms that want a clear read before they commit to a redesign.

Professional services firms of roughly 50 to 300 people that have outgrown the structure they were built on, or whose practice and operational sides are pulling in different directions. It suits partners who want an independent, written assessment of the firm before deciding what to change.

What we look at

All four steps of the Approach, at the level of a recommendation.

  • Assess. How decisions get made and where they stall, where practice and operations pull apart, and which structures and habits are carrying the firm and should be kept. We hear from partners, other professionals, and staff.
  • Quantify. What the strain costs in partner time, decision delay, and turnover, expressed as conservative ranges with the assumptions written out. How we quantify
  • Design. The structural changes that would close the gap, in priority order.
  • Sustain. The governance that would hold those changes in place.

What you get

A written, dated report that belongs to the firm. It describes the structural picture, including the strengths worth keeping, puts a number on what the strain costs, recommends the changes, and says plainly what should come next.

When the findings call for it, the report names Firm Architecture or Firm Alignment as the next step. When they don't, the report stands on its own and the firm has a clear plan to carry out itself.

Questions
Why a fixed fee?

The Firm Evaluation costs $15,000 for every firm. A single price keeps the conversation on the firm and its situation, and the firm knows the full cost before the work starts.

Who does the work?

Paul Thallner leads every Firm Evaluation himself, from the first interview to the final report.

Who owns the report?

The firm does. It is written for the partners and is theirs to use, share, and act on.

Does the Evaluation commit us to more work?

No. The report is complete on its own. If it recommends Firm Architecture or Firm Alignment, that is a separate decision, made with the findings in hand.

What happens after the report?

The partners receive the findings and the recommendation together. If a delivery engagement makes sense, it is scoped from what the Evaluation found.
