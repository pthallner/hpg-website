Who it is for

Firms that have seen the structural picture and decided to rebuild.

Firm Architecture follows the Firm Evaluation. The usual triggers are a long-serving administrative leader retiring, a merger that joined two ways of operating, or growth past the size at which the current model works.

What is included

A new operating model and the governance to keep it.

  • Design of the new operating model around how the firm works now.
  • A map of decision rights and accountability, so it is clear who owns which calls.
  • A transition sequence with owners and dependencies, paced so the firm can absorb the change.
  • Facilitated work with partners and operational leaders to agree on the design.
  • Implementation advice through the first phase of the transition.
  • Governance that holds the design in place: who owns what, which signals show drift, and what prompts a correction.

Why it holds

The people who will run the new model help design it. That takes more time at the start and pays off afterward, because the firm understands the reasoning behind every change and can defend it under pressure.

Client work

Gross McGinley

Law firm, Firm Architecture

Gross McGinley engaged High Peaks Group to redesign its administrative operating model after the retirement of a long-serving administrator. The work covers knowledge transfer and handoffs, and a map of decision rights and accountability built for the firm's next stage of growth.
