Operating model redesign

Firm Architecture

For firms ready to rebuild the operating model they've outgrown.

Delivery engagement · Operating model redesign

Firm Architecture is for firms that have seen the structural picture clearly and are ready to act on it. We redesign the operating model around the firm you have become, and build the governance that keeps the new design in place.

What we do

  • Redesign the operating model around how the firm actually works now.
  • Map decision rights and accountability, so ownership is a design choice, not a personality trait.
  • Sequence the transition, so the change is something the firm can absorb.
  • Build the governance that holds the new design: who owns what, what signals drift, what triggers a course correction.
A new operating model the firm can own, and the governance to keep it.

Why it holds

The redesign is co-created with the people who will live inside it. A model handed down from outside gets quietly worked around. A model the firm helped build is one the firm defends. That is what makes the change last after we leave.
