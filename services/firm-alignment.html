Whole-firm visioning

Firm Alignment

Firm Alignment helps a firm's practice and operational sides agree on where the firm is going, and keeps them there.

Starts after
The Firm Evaluation
Fee
Fixed, scoped from the Evaluation
Led by
Paul Thallner
Who it is for

Firms at a turning point that need everyone pointed the same way.

Firm Alignment suits firms facing a strategic turning point, an ownership transition, or groups that have drifted apart and are starting to cost the firm in coordination and growth. The practice side and the operational side are each capable, and they are pulling in different directions.

What is included

A shared direction the whole firm helped set.

  • A visioning process that involves the whole firm.
  • A way for voices that usually go unheard to reach the partners.
  • Strategic priorities set together, including what to start, stop, and accelerate.
  • Partner adoption of the shared direction.
  • Governance that keeps the direction in place as the firm grows.

Why it holds

Direction set by the whole firm has more owners than direction set by the partners alone. The governance built in at the end gives those owners a way to keep it on course a year later.

Client work

HighSwartz LLP

Law firm, Firm Alignment

HighSwartz brought the whole firm together to set a direction on growth, visibility, and diversity. Attorneys and staff set start, stop, and accelerate priorities together, and the partners adopted the top recommendations from the wider group.

Jay Peak Resort

Hospitality, Firm Alignment

We built Jay Peak's first strategic plan for its new ownership group, with a new operating model and resort-wide goals. The leadership team went on to exceed its targets for labor-to-sales ratio, gross revenue, and guest satisfaction. Jay Peak still works with High Peaks Group on annual planning.
