Paul Thallner

Founder of High Peaks Group. Paul leads every Firm Evaluation himself.

Paul has spent his career on organizational work at scale. As a Principal at Daggerwing Group he led large transformation engagements for Takeda, AT&T, and MasterCard. As a Partner at Great Place to Work he helped organizations understand what high-performing cultures look like from the inside. Earlier, he worked at the Teleos Leadership Institute.

He founded High Peaks Group to bring that experience to mid-sized professional services firms, which are large enough to need deliberate structure and small enough to change it.

He goes into every engagement assuming the people in the firm already hold most of the answers it needs. His job is to design the conditions that let those answers surface, and to put a defensible number on what is at stake.

Thallner is an old Austrian word for guide. Paul's father was Austrian, and Paul grew up skiing in the Alps.

  • Master of Science in Leadership and Organizational Change, Weatherhead School of Management, Case Western Reserve University, where he trained under Richard Boyatzis
  • ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC)
  • Author of Reinventing Resilience
  • Speaker to professional services audiences, including the Association of Legal Administrators
