Client work

Here are three of the organizations we have worked with, what each one asked us to do, and what came of it.

Gross McGinley

Law firm, Firm Architecture

The work began with a structured offsite. A fishbowl format brought non-partner attorneys' views on mentoring, transparency, and technology adoption into the partners' conversation, and the partners adopted the recommendations the group developed together.

When the firm's administrative leader of eighteen years retired, High Peaks Group took on the operating model. The work has covered the firm's administrative capabilities, peer firm structures, knowledge transfer and handoffs, and a map of decision rights and accountability for the next stage of growth.

HighSwartz LLP

Law firm, Firm Alignment

HighSwartz brought the whole firm together to set a direction on growth, visibility, and diversity. Attorneys and staff set start, stop, and accelerate priorities together, and the partners adopted the top recommendations from the wider group.

Jay Peak Resort

Hospitality, Firm Alignment

We built Jay Peak's first strategic plan for its new ownership group, with a new operating model and resort-wide goals. The leadership team went on to exceed its targets for labor-to-sales ratio, gross revenue, and guest satisfaction. Jay Peak still works with High Peaks Group on annual planning.

Before High Peaks Group

Paul Thallner led large-scale transformation engagements as a Principal at Daggerwing Group for clients including Takeda, AT&T, and MasterCard, and was a Partner at Great Place to Work. That work was done for those firms and their clients, and it shaped how High Peaks Group approaches structural change.
