Why look back

2026 asked a lot of how firms are built.

High Peaks Group has tracked the research on professional services firms since July, and this year the pressure came from several directions at once. AI moved into daily work faster than many firms' plans for it. Mergers reshaped the competition, and for some firms raised questions about their own future. More senior people moved toward retirement while fewer new people came into the professions. Midsize firms grew their rates more slowly than the largest firms, and routine work kept eating into margins.

Each of these forces tests the same thing: how a firm makes decisions, divides its work, and spends partner time. A firm can meet every one of them well and still find its structure was built for an earlier version of itself.

Year-end reviews often look at the numbers: revenue, realization, and utilization. The structure that produced those numbers is rarely evaluated. This review gives you a way to see that structure clearly and to put a number on what it is costing your firm in partner time.

How to use it

On your own or with your leadership team.

On your own

About 5 minutes
  1. Think about the firm as you experienced it this year, from your own seat.
  2. Answer three quick questions about your role and firm, then fifteen questions on a sliding scale. Go with your first read.
  3. Read your results, print them, and note one thing to carry into 2027.

With your leadership team

About 5 minutes each
  1. Send this page to each member of your leadership team.
  2. Each person completes the review on their own and prints their results.
  3. Bring the printed results to your next leadership meeting. Compare where your answers line up and where they differ, and start with the question at the end of each report.
