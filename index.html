How we think

The model that built your firm may be holding it back.

When firms grow, their structural design often lags behind. The signs show up as stalled decisions, partners pulled into operational frustrations, good people leaving, or friction between practice areas. The firm responds with a technology investment, another offsite, a chief of staff, or a redesigned bonus pool, and the operating model underneath all of it stays the same.

The firms that move past this point treat their own organization with the rigor they apply to client work. They reassess, redesign the operating model around the firm they have become, and let the old version go.

The core insight

Professional services firms are expert organizations that are rarely expert at being organizations.

The same focus that makes them excellent at client work, and at building lasting relationships, can leave a blind spot for the business decisions that prepare a firm to grow and change.

When firms call us

Two moments bring firms to us.

Growth

You've grown in volume, complexity, or size, and the structure that got you here is showing its limits.

Friction
